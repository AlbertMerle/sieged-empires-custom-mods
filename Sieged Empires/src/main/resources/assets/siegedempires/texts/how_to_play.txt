Sieged Empires is a Minecraft Experience where you can create your own Town, and Join or Create an Empire to conquer the world!-
All nations big and small, have a fair advantage due to our balanced and game-changing war system!-
Whether you're one person, or a nation, you have endless possibilities!-
Explore our world with Views as far as you can see!-
Build NEW Ships and Engage in Naval Combat!-
Trade with your Neighbors, and sail across the Seas!-
Create Locks to safe your items, or picks to break in and steal!-
Discover new Animals and Fend off dangerous Predators!-
Hunt Realistic Animals and Experience Realistic Food scarcity!-
Experience the Harsh Conditions with Temperature, Thirst, and Seasons!-
Chat with Allies and Enemies with Proximity Voice Chat!-
-
The Possibilities are ENDLESS in this world, without straying too far from Vanilla!-
